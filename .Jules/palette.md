## 2025-05-14 - High-Contrast HUD for Vision Systems
**Learning:** In computer vision applications, text overlays (HUDs) can become illegible when the background video content has similar colors or high complexity. Using a semi-transparent dark rectangle behind text ensures accessibility for all users regardless of lighting conditions.
**Action:** Always implement an alpha-blended background for HUD text in OpenCV-based interfaces to maintain a minimum contrast ratio.
